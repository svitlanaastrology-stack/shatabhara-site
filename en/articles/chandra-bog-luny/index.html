Chandra: the Moon god with 27 wives

Based on the Navagraha Purana

In Vedic astrology, behind every planet stands a divine personality.

Today let's meet Chandra — the deity of the Moon.

And believe me, his story is a vivid one.

Chandra is married to 27 daughters of Prajapati Daksha at once. And this is no random number. His 27 wives are the 27 nakshatras the Moon passes through.

Each nakshatra has its own character and energy, and passes it on to a person when the Moon is in it at the moment of birth.

But of all his wives, Chandra loved Rohini most and spent more time with her.

The other sisters took offence and complained to their father. Daksha grew angry and cursed Chandra — he began to lose his strength and fade.

Chandra turned to Shiva, and the curse was softened. That is how the Moon came to wane and then wax again.

But Chandra has one more interesting story.

While studying with his guru Brihaspati — Jupiter, Chandra fell in love with his wife Tara. From their union Budha — Mercury was born.

And here mythology explains astrology very beautifully.

Chandra is our mind: our feelings, emotions, desires and reactions.

And his son Mercury is our intellect: the ability to analyse, discern, draw conclusions and be aware of what is happening to us.

The Moon says:
"I feel."

And Mercury asks:
"Why do I feel this, and what should I do about it?"

Interestingly, in Jyotish the Moon considers Mercury a friend, while Mercury considers the Moon an enemy.

That is Chandra: loving, emotional, changeable, able to lose his strength and gain it again.

Perhaps that is exactly why the Moon tells us so much about our inner world — about how we experience life inside ourselves.
