Series: Rahu and Ketu transit through the rising signs

Transit · Simply about the complex

Rahu in Aquarius — Ketu in Leo

For those with Aries lagna

Time to take your personal creativity out into the wider world

Let's continue with the transits of Rahu and Ketu through the rising signs.

For Aries, an interesting axis is forming right now:

Rahu moves through the 11th house in Aquarius,
Ketu through the 5th house in Leo.

And the axis itself shows the direction of movement:

5th house → 11th house
"I create" → "I show it to the world."

Rahu in the 11th house is considered one of the relatively favourable transits. But remember: the result always depends on the natal chart — on the position of Rahu itself, its strength, its connections and your current periods.

What does Rahu in the 11th house bring?

The 11th house is income, fulfilment of desires, big projects, like-minded people, audience, social connections, the internet and scaling up.

Here Rahu seems to tell Aries:

"It's no longer enough just to be skilled. Make sure others find out what you can do."

This is a time when your circle and your audience can grow, when useful acquaintances, new projects and unconventional ways of earning can appear.

Social media, the internet, technology, working with a large audience and group projects can become especially interesting.

The main task is not to miss opportunities to increase your income and widen your influence.

And what is happening in the 5th house?

Here Ketu is moving through Leo.

The 5th house is creativity, talents, intellect, your own projects, children, romance — the ability to create something of your own.

Ketu often makes us reassess what has already been created, finish what was left undone and stop looking for proof of our worth only in other people's approval.

So the task of this transit is not to give up your creativity.

Quite the opposite.

To look at it with new eyes and understand its real value.

  • What can I already do?
  • What have I created?
  • Where does my talent lie?
  • What is it time to stop hiding and show to more people?

And then — carry the resources of the 5th house into the 11th.

  • Talent → to an audience.
  • Knowledge → to a community.
  • Creativity → into a project.
  • An idea → to scale.
  • A project → into income.

✦ For Aries, this transit can become a time of expansion.

You don't have to create something completely new. Perhaps the most valuable thing has already been created.

Now the task is to give it scale.

And before Rahu's transit through Aquarius ends, try not to miss the opportunities the 11th house opens up.

Don't hide what you can do.
Let the world see it.
