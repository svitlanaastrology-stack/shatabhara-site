In plain words

What Vedic astrology (Jyotish) is

Jyotish is ancient Indian astrology. The word means "the science of light". It looks at where the Sun, the Moon and the planets were at the moment of your birth, and helps you understand your character, your strengths and good periods for important decisions.

How it differs from familiar "Western" astrology

Western astrology counts the signs by the seasons. Vedic astrology counts them by the actual position of the stars in the sky. Over many centuries the sky has "shifted", so your sign in Jyotish may turn out to be different. For example, in Western terms you are a Leo, but in Vedic terms a Cancer. That is normal and often explains a lot.

What you can learn

  • Why the same situations keep repeating in your relationships
  • What your natural talents are and what work suits you
  • Which periods of life are good for moving, marriage or changing jobs
  • How best to support your child, taking their character into account

A few words you will hear in a consultation

Natal chart
A "snapshot" of the sky at the minute of your birth. Like a fingerprint — everyone has their own.
Signs
12 sections of the sky, like 12 rooms with a different "atmosphere": calm in one, lively in another.
Planets
Different sides of your life and character: the Moon is feelings, Venus is love, Saturn is patience and work.
Houses
12 areas of life: family, money, work, health, partner, children and more.
Lagna (ascendant)
The sign that was rising in the east when you were born. It shows how you express yourself in life. It requires an exact birth time.
Nakshatra
The sky is also divided into 27 "lunar mansions". Your nakshatra is the fine-tuning of your character, like the shade of a favourite colour.

Saturn and the Moon

Sade Sati: what this Saturn period is

This is one of my favourite topics: all about Saturn and its influence on our Moon. In Jyotish the Moon is our mind, emotions and desires.

Saturn's transit over the Moon: about 7.5 years

A transit is when a planet, in the real sky right now, moves through the signs of your chart. Sade Sati begins when Saturn enters the sign before your Moon sign and lasts about 7.5 years: Saturn spends roughly 2.5 years in the sign before the Moon, in the Moon's own sign and in the sign after it.

The phases of Sade Sati

  1. 1 ≈ 2.5 years · sign before the Moon

    Relationships and ties

    Relationships and ties may fall apart. A person may lose loved ones, friends, a job or a partner and start hiding from relationships.

  2. 2 ≈ 2.5 years · the Moon's sign

    Health

    Physical health suffers most often. At this time it is especially important to take care of yourself.

  3. 3 ≈ 2.5 years · sign after the Moon

    Support and confidence

    Your material foundation and self-confidence may be shaken.

It is a very hard, but also a very productive period.

"Lifelong" Sade Sati in the natal chart

Sometimes Sade Sati is present in the birth chart itself — it is called "lifelong". Such a person has a special character and frame of mind. There is often a feeling of pressure, a tightness in the chest, and the person doesn't understand where it comes from.

Why we need Saturn

We often live from the false ego and think we were born only to enjoy ourselves and be happy. Joy is needed too. But the main task we came here with is to work and create the best version of ourselves. And no one helps with this as productively as Saturn. It is a hard lesson, but it helps us look inside and ask ourselves questions.

My experience

Many clients who come to me have Sade Sati in their chart.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about consultations

How does an online consultation with a Vedic astrologer work?

You send me your date, time and place of birth, I study your chart in advance, and then we meet by video call. You ask your questions, I answer in plain words and give clear advice. You keep the recording of the session.

What do I need for a Jyotish consultation?

Your date, time and place (city) of birth. For couple compatibility — the same details for both partners. For a children's horoscope — the child's details.

What if I don't know my exact birth time?

Write what you do know: “in the morning”, “around lunchtime” — that already helps. A lot can be seen even without the exact time. Sometimes the time can be refined using important events in your life.

How can I find out a couple's compatibility in Vedic astrology?

I need the birth details of both. In an online synastry reading I compare the two charts and tell you where you support each other, where friction is possible and how to make the relationship warmer. Your partner doesn't have to be present.

What is the Navamsha (D9) chart and why read it?

It is an additional chart built from the same birth data. It helps to see relationships more clearly: how you should best behave with people, and especially in marriage. I read the D9 navamsha together with the main chart — this way marriage compatibility in Jyotish can be seen more deeply.

Can a family-relationships astrologer help if a marriage is going through difficulties?

Yes. I look at both partners' charts and the Navamsha chart. A marriage partner is our mirror: the reading helps you see your own sore spots and understand how to work with them. I have been married for 37 years myself and know this subject from the inside.

Can I find my life purpose through my natal chart?

Yes. The chart shows your natural talents and the kind of work that gives you joy and strength. In Jyotish this is called svadharma — “your own work”. We turn it into concrete steps: what to try, what to learn.

How can I see in my natal chart where my money is?

In Jyotish, money and prosperity are called “artha”. They are shown by four houses of the chart: the 2nd — savings, the 6th — daily work, the 10th — career, the 11th — income. They show where your money comes from, which periods are good for earning and how to keep money.

How can a young person find themselves through the natal chart?

The chart shows your talents, your strengths and the work you will enjoy. As an astrologer for young people, I help with choosing a profession through Jyotish, point out the fears that hold you back and the possibilities your horoscope offers.

How do I know whether I have Sade Sati?

I need your date, time and place of birth. From them I see which sign your Moon is in and where Saturn is now — this shows whether you are in Sade Sati, which phase and when it will end. I also check whether there is a “lifelong” Sade Sati in the natal chart.

Is Sade Sati dangerous?

No, it is not a sentence. The period can be hard, but it is very productive: Saturn teaches wisdom and discipline and helps you look inside yourself. In a consultation we work out how to go through it more calmly and with benefit.

How is a children's horoscope in Vedic astrology useful for parents?

It helps you understand your child's character better: what inspires them, what frightens them, what they are talented at and how to talk to them. It is not a forecast of “who they will become”, but a hint on how to support them.

Can an astrologer help me choose a country or city to move to?

Yes. The chart can show in which places it will be easier for you to live and work, and help pick a good time to move. This is especially useful if you are choosing between several options.

Yes, I am from Ukraine and I consult online, so it doesn't matter where you live. All you need is the internet and a convenient messenger.

How is Vedic astrology different from Western astrology?

Vedic astrology counts the signs by the actual position of the stars in the sky, while Western astrology counts them by the seasons. So your sign may be different. Jyotish also pays a lot of attention to the Moon and to the periods of life — when it is best to act.
